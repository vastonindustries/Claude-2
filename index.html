FAQ

Good to know

Is there online payment?

Not yet. You submit your design and quantity, and Vaston reviews it and replies with a quotation. Payment and order confirmation are arranged with you directly.

Can I order just one piece?

Yes. You can request a single customised item or a bulk quantity in the same enquiry.

Is the 3D preview exactly what I will receive?

It is a visual preview to help you design. Final colours, fabric and print finish may vary slightly from the screen.
